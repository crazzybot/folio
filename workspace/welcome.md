# Welcome

Welcome to **Markdown Previewer** — a lightweight tool for browsing and reading
Markdown documents right in your browser.

Use the panel on the left to navigate between documents. Click any file to render
it instantly in the viewer on the right. You can drag the divider to resize the
panels, or collapse the sidebar entirely with the `‹` button.

## Getting started

1. Drop any `.md` or `.markdown` files into the `workspace/` folder.
2. Restart (or keep running) the backend — it scans the folder on every request.
3. Refresh the browser tab and your new documents will appear in the list.

Happy writing! ✨
